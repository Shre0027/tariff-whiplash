# 2-minute walkthrough: script

> For a screen recording (Loom or similar) to put at the top of the README and on LinkedIn. Speak naturally; this is a guide, not something to read word for word. Practice it 2–3 times first.

**Setup:** browser at full screen, the story page open at the top. Close other tabs. Record your voice; your face in a small corner is a bonus.

| Time | On screen | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Story page, top | "In 2025, the US announced tariffs of 10, 25, even 145 percent. I wanted to know what importers actually paid, so I built this from the Census Bureau's own trade data." |
| 0:15–0:40 | Scroll through the rate chart | "This is the rate actually charged at the border. It went from 2.2 to almost 11 percent, then the Supreme Court struck most of it down. China's headline was 145 percent; what was actually charged peaked at 48, because so many goods were exempt." |
| 0:40–0:55 | Scroll to "Product by product" | "And buying moved fast. Two-thirds of laptops came from China in 2024; now it's 7 percent, mostly Vietnam." |
| 0:55–1:25 | Open Tariff Desk → Tariff rules → run the what-if | "Then I asked: what would an importer actually need? I wrote the requirements and built this prototype. If a new 15 percent tariff hits Vietnam, it shows which products are affected and which fall below the margin target, instantly." |
| 1:25–1:40 | Shipments & refunds, filter "Deadline ≤14 days" | "It also tracks the refund for every shipment, because each one has its own deadline, and missing it can mean going to court." |
| 1:40–2:00 | Rule validation page | "The part I'm proudest of: I checked the tariff rules against what Census shows was actually charged, about 2,000 times. The first version was off in places; the gaps showed me missing rules. Now the median error is 0.3 points. The company in the tracker is sample data; everything else is real. Links are below." |

**After recording:** trim the start and end, add the link to the README (top) and to the project on LinkedIn.
