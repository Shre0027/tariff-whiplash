# Stakeholder interviews

> **Status: planned, not yet done.** The requirements and pain points in this project come from research (legal texts, trade publications and the data). This page is the plan for checking them with people who do the work. Findings will be added below and fed back into the [requirements](requirements.md) and [process maps](process-maps.md), with the source of each change.

## Why
Every requirement in Part B was written from reading, not from users. Two or three 15-minute conversations can confirm the biggest pain points, find ones the research missed, and show which features matter. Changing the requirements because of what users said is the core of a BA's job.

## Outreach message (LinkedIn)

> Hi [Name], I'm a business analyst in Boston working on a portfolio project about the 2025–26 tariff changes and the IEEPA refund process. I've built a prototype tracker for importers and would value 15 minutes of your perspective on what was hardest to manage, from someone who did it for real. No sales, nothing confidential needed, and I'll keep your name and company out of anything I publish. Would you be open to a short call next week?

## Interview guide (15 minutes)

**Opening (1 min)**
- Thank them; confirm 15 minutes; confirm you won't publish their name or company, and ask if you may quote them anonymously ("a licensed customs broker in Boston").

**Their work (2 min)**
1. What's your role in getting goods through customs?
2. Roughly how many entries or SKUs do you handle?

**Tariff changes (4 min)** · checks pain points P1–P4
3. When a tariff changed in 2025, what happened in the first 48 hours? Who did what?
4. What was hardest: knowing which products were affected, calculating the cost, or something else?
5. How did you work out the new landed cost? (Spreadsheet? ERP? Broker?)
6. Did anyone change suppliers or countries because of it? What made it worth it, or not?

**Refunds (4 min)** · checks rules R1–R8 and FR-5 to FR-9
7. How are you handling IEEPA refunds: CAPE, protests, court?
8. What has gone wrong or been slow? (Rejected declarations, ACH setup, finding entries, deadlines)
9. How do you keep track of the 80-day and 180-day deadlines today?

**Show the prototype (3 min)**
10. Share the tracker. "If you had this in 2025, what would you use first? What's missing? What's wrong?"

**Close (1 min)**
11. Anything I should have asked?
12. Is there anyone else you'd suggest I talk to?

**Tips:** ask "how" and "what happened" rather than "would you like". Let silences run. Write down their exact words for the strongest points.
