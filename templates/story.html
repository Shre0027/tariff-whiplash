01 · The real rate

Headline tariffs said 145%. The border said something else.

A tariff announcement names a rate. What importers pay depends on which goods it covers, the exemptions and how it stacks with other duties. This chart shows the rate actually charged: duty divided by the value of everything imported, month by month.

07 · Getting the money back

A $166 billion refund, one shipment at a time

Because the emergency tariffs were unlawful, importers can get them back. Customs (CBP) built a new tool, CAPE, to process refunds in bulk. By 2 October 2026 about — had been sent for payment.

But each shipment ("entry") has its own route, depending on whether customs has finalized ("liquidated") its duty and how long ago. Miss a deadline and the money may need a lawsuit. See the full process map.

Method

Effective rate = calculated duty ÷ imports for consumption. Calculated duty is assessed at entry; refunds paid later are not subtracted. Every number on this page comes from the same SQL queries as the analysis.
